# LinkedIn Banner (4:1 Safe Zone Optimized)

Bhai, maine issue samajh liya hai. LinkedIn 4:1 aspect ratio use karta hai, isliye pehle wale banners crop ho rahe the.

Maine ek naya banner design kiya hai jisme saara content ek **Narrow Middle Strip** (Safe Zone) mein hai. Jab aap isey LinkedIn pe upload karke crop karenge, toh kuch bhi cut nahi hoga.

### [Download 4:1 Optimized Banner](https://ais-dev-isv25bagvu3nzqunew6sgw-894858191672.asia-east1.run.app/src/assets/images/shivank_linkedin_banner_4to1_safe_1790966498533.jpg)

### Why this works:
- **Aspect Ratio**: Maine content ko sirf middle band mein rakha hai.
- **LinkedIn Cropping**: LinkedIn upload ke waqt aap ise center karke fit kar payenge, aur saare tech icons aur naam ekdum perfect dikhenge.
- **RTL Icon**: Isme React Testing Library ka official goat icon hi use kiya hai.

### How to Save:
1. Link pe click karke use open karein.
2. Image par **Right-Click** karke **"Save Image As..."** select karein.
3. Ya phir app ke andar jo **Floating Icon** hai, wahan se download kar lijiye, maine wahan bhi link update kar diya hai.

*(Note: Upload ke waqt LinkedIn ka 'Zoom' slider use karke use exactly adjust kar lena.)*
